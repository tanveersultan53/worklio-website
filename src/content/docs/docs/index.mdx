Worklio is a team chat you run on your own servers. Your team gets channels, DMs and threads, ringing calls and huddles, a live whiteboard, collaborative docs, boards, voice and video clips, and an AI assistant that only sees what the person asking is allowed to see.

It's MIT licensed: free to use, change and build on.

![The #general channel in Worklio, with reactions, a thread, a poll and a pinned message](/screenshots/channel.webp)

## What you can do with it

- **Talk:** channels, DMs, group DMs and threads, with reactions, polls, pins, bookmarks, scheduled messages and search filters.
- **Meet:** ring a teammate or start a huddle. Share your screen and draw on it, take notes, chat, and blur your background.
- **Make:** write docs together in canvases, sketch on a whiteboard, and plan work on boards with priorities, labels and due dates.
- **Ask:** Worklio AI answers questions about your workspace with sources, catches you up on channels and turns threads into tasks.
- **Run it:** roles and permissions, invite links, an audit log, and one Docker Compose file for production.
