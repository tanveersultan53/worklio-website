Every screen in Worklio, captured at 1920 × 1080 from a production build using the fictional demo workspace. Lossless PNG originals are in the [repository](https://github.com/tanveersultan53/worklio/tree/main/docs/screenshots).
## Getting started

### Sign in

Email and password with a show-password toggle, Caps Lock warning and the last email remembered. Sessions renew in the background for 30 days.

![Sign in](/screenshots/sign-in.webp)

### Create an account

Full name, work email and a password with a live strength meter.

![Create an account](/screenshots/sign-up.webp)

### Create a workspace

Name your workspace; its URL is generated from the name automatically.

![Create a workspace](/screenshots/create-workspace.webp)

### Join from an invite link

Shows who invited you and to which workspace; a new person creates an account and joins in one step.

![Join from an invite link](/screenshots/invite.webp)

## Messaging

### Channel

Messages with reactions, thread summaries, a live poll, pinned and saved messages, a bookmarks bar, and tabs for Messages, Canvas, Board, Whiteboard and Files.

![Channel](/screenshots/channel.webp)

### Thread

Replies open beside the conversation with their own composer.

![Thread](/screenshots/thread.webp)

### Message actions

Quick reactions, reply in thread, save for later, pin, remind me, forward, unfollow, copy link and copy text.

![Message actions](/screenshots/message-actions.webp)

### Emoji reactions

Frequently used emoji first, plus a full picker with search.

![Emoji reactions](/screenshots/reactions.webp)

### Slash commands

Type / for /remind, /poll, /schedule, /dnd, /status, /away and more.

![Slash commands](/screenshots/slash-commands.webp)

### Schedule a message

Send tomorrow at 9:00, next Monday at 9:00, or at a custom time.

![Schedule a message](/screenshots/schedule-send.webp)

### Pinned messages

Every pinned message in the channel, with who pinned it.

![Pinned messages](/screenshots/pins.webp)

### Search with filters

Filter by person, channel, content type and date; matches are highlighted.

![Search with filters](/screenshots/search.webp)

### Quick switcher (⌘K)

Jump to any channel, person, canvas or view, or search all messages.

![Quick switcher (⌘K)](/screenshots/quick-switcher.webp)

### Direct message

One-to-one conversation with the person's title and status, and audio and video call buttons.

![Direct message](/screenshots/direct-message.webp)

### Channel details

About, Members, Integrations and Settings for each channel.

![Channel details](/screenshots/channel-details.webp)
