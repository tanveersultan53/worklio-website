Every channel has three shared spaces next to its messages.

## Canvas

A document the whole channel edits together, live. Several people can type at once and see each other's changes and cursors.

- Headings, lists, checklists, tables, quotes, code, images and @mentions.
- Type `/` for the block menu.
- Comments on any part of the text.
- Start from a template, or browse every canvas in **Canvases**.

![A canvas with a launch plan](/screenshots/canvas.webp)

## Whiteboard

A shared drawing surface (built on Excalidraw) with live cursors. Use it on its own or bring it onto the stage during a call.

![A whiteboard with a launch diagram](/screenshots/whiteboard.webp)

## Board

Plan work as cards in columns.

- Assign people, set **priority**, **labels**, **due dates** and a cover colour.
- Drag cards and columns, and add custom fields (status, priority, people, date, number, select).
- Switch to the **table view** to sort, group and filter.
- Cards have **subtasks** and **comments**.
