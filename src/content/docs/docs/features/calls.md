Worklio uses [LiveKit](https://livekit.io/) for audio and video. It runs as part of your deployment, so calls stay on your infrastructure.

## Calls

Start an audio or video call from a DM or a person's profile. The other person's Worklio **rings**, with an incoming-call window they can accept or decline. Missed, declined and cancelled calls leave a card in the conversation.

![An incoming video call](/screenshots/incoming-call.webp)

## Huddles

A huddle is a call that lives in a channel. Anyone in the channel can drop in.

- Members get a banner and a chime when a huddle starts (respecting mute, Do Not Disturb and notification settings).
- The sidebar shows which channels have a live huddle.
- Past huddles, with duration and people, are listed in **Huddles**.

## In the meeting window

- A **grid** of participants, or switch the stage to a **whiteboard**, a **board** or a **shared screen**.
- **Share your screen and draw on it** with a pen, arrows, shapes or a laser pointer.
- **Notes** that are posted to the thread when the call ends, **in-call chat**, **raise hand** and **reactions**.
- **Live captions** and a transcript, using your browser's speech recognition.
- **Camera effects**: background blur and virtual backgrounds, with a camera check before you join.

With Worklio AI turned on, a huddle can produce a **summary with action items**.
