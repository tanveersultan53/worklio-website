title: Admin and permissions
description: Roles, invitations, workspace settings, permissions and the audit log in Worklio.
---

Workspace owners and admins manage Worklio from **Workspace settings** (the gear at the top of the sidebar).

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including transferring ownership |
| **Admin** | Manage members, channels, permissions, apps and AI settings |
| **Member** | Use public channels and everyday features |
| **Guest** | Only see the channels they were invited to |

## Members

Change roles, **deactivate** someone (they can't sign in to the workspace; their messages stay), reactivate them, or remove them.

## Invitations

Create **invite links** that join people as members or guests, with an expiry and a maximum number of uses. Revoke a link at any time. People who already have an account can also be added by email.

## Permissions

Decide who can create public and private channels, invite people, use AI and more. Permissions are enforced on the server, not just hidden in the interface.

## Workspace settings

Name, icon, description, workspace URL (generated from the name when the workspace is created) and default channels for new members.

## Audit log

Every sensitive change (roles, access, settings, invitations, AI and apps) is recorded with who did it and when. API keys are never logged.
